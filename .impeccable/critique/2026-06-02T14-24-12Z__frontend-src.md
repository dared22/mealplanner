## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Mid-tier AI slop. The category-reflex check fails at both altitudes:
- First-order (meal planner / healthy living): forest green `#3D5A3D` + sage `#E8F4E8` + cream gradients + Playfair Display serif. Zero attempt to escape the wellness palette.
- Second-order (avoiding the obvious wellness reflex usually lands on "Notion-meets-Headspace editorial-typographic"): the LeftPanel does exactly this with `<h1>About <span className="accent">yourself</span></h1>` patterns. Both traps caught.

Bigger tells: step titles padded for symmetry ("Foundation of Growth / Energy Calibration / Goal Alignment / Dietary Intelligence / Flavor Profile / Lifestyle Fit"), every step a grid of identical cards (4, 5, 8, 10), results page lands on a hero-metric template, twin spinners during a 30-120s wait.

**Deterministic scan.** Bundled detector module is missing on this machine; CLI scan returned exit 1 (not findings exit). Supplemental greps:
- Side-stripe borders: 0. Gradient text: 0. Glassmorphism: 0. Em-dash in `translations.js`: 0.
- Pure white/black Tailwind classes: 6 (`DietaryStep.jsx:96`, `ResultsStep.jsx:515,727,729`, `Login.jsx:49`, `Recipes.jsx:54`), all foreground/scrim usage.
- Literal `#ffffff` in CSS: 1 (`index.css:60`, dead Vite-template code).
- Hand-rolled modal: 1 (`ResultsStep.jsx:515`, no `role="dialog"`, no focus trap, no Esc).

Where A and B diverge: em dashes are clean in `translations.js` but present in JSX text (`ResultsStep.jsx:116` GenerationBadge, step subtitles in `MealPlanner.jsx:60-87`) — direct absolute-ban violations the grep missed. Neutral palette in `styles.css` uses raw greys (`#FAFAFA`, `#1A1A1A`, `#666666`) with zero hue tint — violates "tint every neutral".

**Visual overlays.** Skipped: dev server not running, no live inspection requested.

## Overall Impression

Solid bones (versioned localStorage resume, real inline validation, full i18n discipline) hidden under a wellness-template skin the codebase keeps reinforcing. Every surface reaches for the same vocabulary: serif headline, accent-colored last word, grid of identical cards, sage backdrop. Biggest opportunity: the plan-generation wait, currently the worst moment in the app, could be the strongest if it streamed partial results or anchored to the user's own choices.

## What's Working

1. **PersonalInfoStep validation (`PersonalInfoStep.jsx:24-33, 64-153`)** — inline, surfaced only after interaction, cross-field logic checks for BMI-impossible combos. Above the bar for early-stage product UI.
2. **State persistence (`MealPlanner.jsx:29-53, 433-445`)** — versioned, user-namespaced localStorage covering step, form, plan payload and status, with graceful JSON-parse failure. Users can close the tab mid-generation and resume.
3. **i18n discipline (`translations.js`)** — every user-visible string in the core flow goes through `t()`, including dynamic option titles. Norwegian occasionally clinical but the wiring is complete.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Twin spinners and dead air during a 30-120s plan-generation wait
- **Why it matters.** `ResultsStep.jsx:725-731` puts a spinning `RefreshCw` inside the hero circle; `ResultsStep.jsx:776` renders a second 64×64 border-spinner directly below. Polling 60 × 2s = up to 120s. No estimate, no anchoring to user inputs, no progressive reveal, no escape, no "we'll save it if you close the tab". High abandonment risk at the conversion moment.
- **Fix.** Kill one spinner. Replace stage label with an elapsed-time progress arc. Show the profile being planned against ("28y, lose weight, Mediterranean + Asian, 30-60 min"). Stream day 1 first, then expand. Add tip/fact rotation. Offer "notify me" past 30s.
- **Suggested command.** `/impeccable animate` + `/impeccable onboard`.

### [P1] Step copy and left-panel narrative read as AI-generated marketing
- **Why it matters.** `MealPlanner.jsx:56-87`: step titles "Foundation of Growth / Energy Calibration / Goal Alignment / Dietary Intelligence / Flavor Profile / Lifestyle Fit" — noun + noun in Title Case with grandiose subtitles. Combined with em dashes inside `GenerationBadge` and step subtitles (absolute-ban violation), reads as a wellness content model, not a tool. Drives the slop verdict more than any visual choice.
- **Fix.** Operational language: "About you / How active are you? / What's your goal? / Any food restrictions? / What do you like to eat? / Cooking time and budget." Strip LeftPanel narrative; it's hidden under `lg` anyway. Replace every `—` with comma, colon, period, or parens.
- **Suggested command.** `/impeccable clarify` then `/impeccable distill`.

### [P1] The identical-card-grid pattern IS the UI vocabulary
- **Why it matters.** Direct absolute-ban violation. Goals (4), Activity (5), Dietary (8), Cuisine (10), DayCarousel, Profile Summary all share `border-2 rounded-2xl + icon + title + description + check`. No structural variety = no hierarchy, no rhythm, no spine.
- **Fix.** Differentiate by intent: Dietary → segmented list with allergen/preference badges (it's filtering); Cuisine → regional chip cloud with grouping and search; Goals → 3 large committed choices; DayCarousel → horizontal timeline with macro spark-bars.
- **Suggested command.** `/impeccable layout` + `/impeccable distill`.

### [P2] Dashboard nav is overstuffed, duplicates JSX, ships a dead search input
- **Why it matters.** `DashboardLayout.jsx` and `MealPlanner.jsx` define two near-identical Header components with subtle drift. `DashboardLayout.jsx:22` wires an `<input>` with no `onChange` — interactive-looking dead control. 8 elements in 72px desktop bar; on mobile `nav-section-center` is `hidden`, leaving the logo as the only way back to `/planner` from `/recipes`.
- **Fix.** Hoist a single `<AppShell>`, remove dead search until it does something, add mobile bottom-nav for the 3 primary destinations.
- **Suggested command.** `/impeccable harden` + `/impeccable adapt`.

### [P3] Raw AI response leaked into production results
- **Why it matters.** `ResultsStep.jsx:893-898` ends the success state with a `<details>` "View AI Response" expanding into `<pre>{rawPlanText}</pre>`. Last visible thing on page. Tells users the product is a thin OpenAI wrapper; potentially exposes prompt structure.
- **Fix.** Remove from production. Gate behind admin/debug flag.
- **Suggested command.** `/impeccable polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Time-pressed parent (project-primary).** Cannot skip CuisineStep with a "Surprise me." Must re-traverse 6 steps to regenerate weekly (or "Retake questionnaire" wipes state). 30-120s wait with twin spinners is intolerable while supervising a child. Groceries is `Under Construction`. The single most valuable feature, "carry-forward leftovers," is buried as the last toggle in step 6 with no explanation.

**Dietary-restriction user.** DietaryStep flattens 8 restrictions into unordered chips; a nut allergy and paleo preference look identical. No free-text "other" for sesame, shellfish, FODMAPs. The "None" toggle silently wipes other selections, no undo. Generated meals are not visibly tagged "vegan-safe" or "contains excluded ingredient".

**Non-Norwegian speaker.** Language toggle is a 2-letter `NO`/`EN` with no globe icon, easy to miss on first load. Mid-flow language switch triggers a refetch via `useEffect([lang])`; user sees the plan disappear with no acknowledgement that their action caused it. Norwegian translations of clinical step titles read bureaucratic in both languages.

## Minor Observations

- `index.css` and `App.css` are dead Vite-template code, contradict `styles.css`.
- `Logo.jsx` exports an emerald-gradient logo that conflicts with the olive `#3D5A3D` brand color; `LogoInline` uses `/logo.png` instead. Two competing logo identities.
- `headline-serif` uses Playfair Display on wizard h1s — display fonts in product UI is brand cosplay.
- `Forbidden.jsx`, `Groceries.jsx` ship raw Tailwind (`text-gray-600`, `text-2xl font-bold`) that bypasses tokens.
- `App.jsx:53` catch-all routes signed-out users to `/login` with no breadcrumb of intended destination.
- `SwapModal` (`ResultsStep.jsx:515`) uses `bg-black/50` raw scrim, no `aria-modal`, no Esc, no focus trap.
- `text-red-500 / text-green-500 / text-orange-500` hardcoded; no `--color-error / --color-success` tokens.
- `transition-all` everywhere animates layout properties.
- `#3D5A3D` literal in `ResultsStep.jsx:84` `MACRO_COLORS` instead of `var(--primary)`.
- Three coupled `useEffect`s `ResultsStep.jsx:614-645` over `activePlan / isLoading / generationStartTime` — fragile.
- Neutral palette in `styles.css` (`#FAFAFA`, `#1A1A1A`, `#666666`) has zero hue tint.

## Questions to Consider

1. Why is plan generation a wait at all? Could step 6 stream day 1 inline as the user finishes selecting budget?
2. Is the six-step questionnaire a moat or a tax? If the differentiator is the recipe DB and solver, can the user start with one question and refine only when it changes the plan?
3. What would Preppr look like without a serif font, without green, and without cards? If the answer is "I don't know", the design has no spine.
